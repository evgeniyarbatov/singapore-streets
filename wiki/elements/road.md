# Road

The most common generic element in Singapore street names.[^3] 1,021 of the 4,787 streets contain it, and in 1,020 of them it is the last word.

## How it is used

The naming board ranks street descriptors from major arterial roads down to local access roads: Expressway > Highway > Boulevard > Avenue > Way > Road > Street > Walk > Lane.[^1] In its table of road categories, Road appears with [[drive|Drive]] and [[street|Street]] for minor arterial and primary access roads.[^1]

Within British military camps, roads were named after famous British streets.[^2]

A location word can follow Road, as in Admiralty Road East and Admiralty Road West; the naming board allows North, South, East, West and Central as descriptors of where a street lies.[^1] Its Malay counterpart is [[jalan|Jalan]], which comes first in the name instead of last.[^3]
