# View

167 of the 4,787 streets contain View, and in 154 it is the last word. Housing & Development (54) is the largest group.

## How it is used

View is not part of the naming board's road hierarchy. The board lists "View / Vista" among descriptors based on a street's locational context, alongside Central, Gate, Terrace and Place.[^1] Its example of a neighbourhood sharing one locality name uses View as one of the suffixes: West Coast with Drive, Avenue, Heights or View.[^1] Seletar Aerospace View appears among its examples of streets named for an estate's industry.[^1]

View can also be part of the specific name rather than the descriptor, as in Tuas View Square and the Tuas View Walk N series.
