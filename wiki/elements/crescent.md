# Crescent

153 of the 4,787 streets contain Crescent, and in 149 it is the last word. Housing & Development (75) is the largest group, about half.

## How it is used

Crescent describes shape, not rank: the naming board says names should reflect a street's configuration, and lists Loop, Crescent and Ring Road for curved roads.[^1] Other shape descriptors on its list include Circle, Circus, Circuit, Oval, Square and Close, the last for cul-de-sacs.[^1] Seletar Aerospace Crescent is one of its examples of streets named for the industry of the estate they serve.[^1]

Crescent can also be the specific name, as in Crescent Road and Crescent View.
