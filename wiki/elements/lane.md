# Lane

214 of the 4,787 streets contain Lane, and it is the last word in all of them once numbers and compass words are set aside. The largest groups are Colonial & British (52) and Chinese Dialect & Clan (50).

## How it is used

Lane is the bottom of the naming board's hierarchy of descriptors: Expressway > Highway > Boulevard > Avenue > Way > [[road|Road]] > [[street|Street]] > [[walk|Walk]] > Lane.[^1] With Walk and Link it is a descriptor for local access roads, the lowest of the five road categories.[^1] Seletar Aerospace Lane is one of the board's examples of streets named for the industry of the estate they serve.[^1]

The Malay word for lane is [[lorong|Lorong]].[^2] Unlike Lane, it usually comes first: 202 of the 208 Lorong streets start with it.
