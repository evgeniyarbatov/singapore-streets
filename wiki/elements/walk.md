# Walk

191 of the 4,787 streets contain Walk, and in 190 it is the last word. Nature & Geography (48) and Housing & Development (45) are the largest groups.

## How it is used

In the naming board's hierarchy, Walk ranks below [[street|Street]] and above [[lane|Lane]], the last two steps of Expressway > Highway > Boulevard > Avenue > Way > [[road|Road]] > Street > Walk > Lane.[^1] With Lane and Link it is a descriptor for local access roads, the lowest of the five road categories.[^1]

Walk often follows a landscape word: the board gives Punggol Field Walk as an example of Field used for streets in low-lying or wooded areas.[^1] Boardwalk is a separate descriptor in the board's list, grouped with bridges, flyovers and tunnels as a name based on function.[^1]
