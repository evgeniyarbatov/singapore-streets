# Avenue

460 of the 4,787 streets end in Avenue. 176 of them fall in the Numeric & Functional category, more than any other.

## How it is used

Avenue sits high in the naming board's hierarchy, below Boulevard and above Way, [[road|Road]] and [[street|Street]].[^1] It is listed with Boulevard, Way and [[drive|Drive]] as a descriptor for major arterial roads.[^1]

Numbers in a name are spelled out, so the street is Sixth Avenue, not 6th Avenue, to avoid confusion with house numbers.[^1] Sixth Avenue belongs to a numbered sequence off Bukit Timah Road. The numbering system it used was later restricted to Housing and Development Board and Jurong Town Corporation estates.[^2]

A location word can follow Avenue, as in Happy Avenue North, Happy Avenue Central, Happy Avenue East and Happy Avenue West.[^1]
